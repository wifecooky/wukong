Character pose sheet for a 2D animated short. The character in the reference images is the Monkey King from our film. Use him as the same character: keep his costume, colours and face design (golden-tan fur hood around the face, red peach-heart-shaped face mask with golden eye paint around golden eyes, cream muzzle, gold crown band with small red pompoms and two very long striped pheasant tail feathers, yellow tunic, teal scarf, red sash belt, short orange tiger-stripe skirt, red trousers, black cloth boots, long curling tail). Do not redesign him. The reference drawings are crude; redraw him as a skilled 2D animator would: appealing, lively, strong silhouettes, clear line of action, real anatomy and weight, expressive face.

Style: clean hand-drawn 2D animation cel, confident dark brown-black ink outline with varying line weight, flat colour with one soft shadow tone, a little Chinese ink-painting feel. No gradients, no texture, no 3D render look.

Layout: exactly 6 full-body poses in a 3 x 2 grid, the same character at the same scale in every cell, every figure facing screen-right in three-quarter side view, each figure fully inside its cell with wide empty space around it, nothing touching or overlapping between cells. Background: one flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) colour everywhere, no floor, no shadows on the ground, no text, no labels, no grid lines. He holds NO staff and NO weapon in any pose.

Top row, left to right:
1. Mid-somersault: curled into a tight ball, knees to chest, arms hugging shins, tail wrapped around, feathers trailing.
2. Impact landing: very deep crouch, left hand slapped flat on the ground in front, right arm flung up behind for balance, head up and eyes sharp, feathers and tail whipping up from the impact.
3. Standing up cocky: upright relaxed stance, weight on the back leg, arms loose, chin up, sly smirk.

Bottom row, left to right:
4. Plucking the needle: right hand raised behind his ear pinching a tiny golden needle, head tilted toward it, mischievous eyes, weight shifting forward.
5. Presenting: deep forward lunge, right arm thrust straight forward at shoulder height with the fist closed and turned as if gripping a horizontal pole (fist empty), left arm swept back, big open-mouthed grin.
6. Planted hero pose: wide firm stance, right fist closed at chest height as if gripping a tall upright pole beside him (fist empty), left hand on hip, chest out, chin up, proud grin, feathers swaying.
