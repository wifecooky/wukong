Expression sheet for the same 2D animated short. The FIRST reference image is our approved model sheet for the Monkey King; the SECOND shows two approved close-up portraits of him. Copy that exact drawing style, line weight, colours, face paint, hood, gold headband with red pompoms and the two long striped pheasant feathers. Same character, same artist. Do not redesign anything.

Style: identical to the references: clean hand-drawn 2D animation cel, confident dark brown-black ink outline with varying line weight, flat colour with one soft shadow tone. No gradients, no texture, no 3D look.

Story beat: he has been trapped for 500 years under a mountain; only his head sticks out of a crack in the rock. So each drawing is ONLY his head in the hood, plus the hood's lower edge and a little of the green scarf at the bottom; no shoulders, no arms, no body.

Layout: exactly 6 drawings in a 3 x 2 grid, each fully inside its own cell with wide empty space around it, nothing touching or crossing between cells. Every head is the SAME size, the SAME three-quarter view facing screen-right, the SAME tilt and the SAME position inside its cell, so they can be swapped frame to frame like animation drawings; only the face changes. Background: one flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, no rock, no ground, no text, no labels, no grid lines.

Top row, left to right:
1. Asleep: eyes fully closed in peaceful curved lines, mouth closed in a faint content smile.
2. Just woken: eyes open, looking down and inward, a little cross-eyed, at the tip of his own nose, brows raised in mild surprise, mouth closed.
3. Blowing: cheeks puffed, lips pushed into a small round "o", blowing a puff of air up, eyes looking down at his nose, brows slightly lowered.

Bottom row, left to right:
4. Awake and alert: eyes wide open, looking straight ahead to the right, brows level, mouth closed, calm and focused.
5. Sly: a knowing closed-mouth grin, one corner of the mouth higher, brows lowered and eyes narrowed with mischief, looking ahead to the right.
6. Fierce effort: huge toothy grin with clenched teeth, brows pulled down hard, eyes wide and intense looking ahead to the right, as if straining to burst the mountain.
